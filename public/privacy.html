Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Study with Buddy ("the App") is a study task and productivity tool for Class 12 and competitive exam students. This page explains what information we collect, how it's used, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

  • Account info: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google to create your account.
  • Study data: Tasks you create, their categories, completion status, and dates, plus your study streak and exam countdown settings.
  • Google Calendar and Google Tasks data: If you connect Google Calendar/Tasks sync, we store an OAuth refresh token so the App can create, update, and delete matching tasks/events on your behalf. This token is stored securely on our server and is never exposed to the browser or other users.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core features: your dashboard, streaks, and the weekly leaderboard (which shows your name, target exam, and weekly completed task count to other signed-in users).
  • To sync tasks you create in the App with Google Tasks and Google Calendar, and to pull back changes you make directly in Google.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use your Google data for advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except with Google's APIs solely to perform the Calendar/Tasks sync you've explicitly enabled. Your weekly task count, name, and target exam are visible to other logged-in users on the leaderboard; no other data is shared between users.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can disconnect Google sync at any time, which stops future syncing. To request full account and data deletion, contact us using the details below.

Security

Data is stored using Supabase with row-level security so only you can access your own tasks and profile data. Google OAuth tokens are stored in a server-only table that is never accessible from the browser or by other users.

Your Choices

  • You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions.
  • You can delete individual tasks, or request full account deletion, at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the developer contact email listed on our Google Cloud OAuth consent screen.
